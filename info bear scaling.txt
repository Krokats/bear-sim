- Haste: 
Haste increases your melee attack speed, and therefore increases the number of Mauls that can be cast in a given encounter. In vanilla WoW, when determining your character's melee swing timer, the game converts each source of % Haste from your gear and buffs into a Haste multiplier, and each of these factors are then multiplied together in order to determine a total attack speed multiplier for scaling your swing timer.

Note that the stat weight for Haste is nearly twice as high for bears as it is for cats. 
This is because almost 100% of a bear's Threat generation is tied 1:1 to the swing timer via Maul. 
In contrast, auto-attacks constitute only ~40% of a cat's DPS output, with the majority coming from instant cast abilities that do not benefit from Haste. 
More generally, bears scale far better with Haste than any other spec in vanilla WoW due to their reliance on Maul.

- Dodge:
Dodge Chance increases the probability that you dodge incoming melee attacks, and is thus exclusively an avoidance stat that reduces a tank's average DTPS from mobs. 
Dodge Chance typically comes in 1-2% increments on gear pieces, and is one of three possible ways to improve your total avoidance as a Feral tank in vanilla, 
the others being Agility (which grants 0.05% Dodge per point) and Defense (which grants 0.04% Dodge per point but also increases the boss' chance to miss by an additional 0.04%).
When gearing as a Feral tank in vanilla, it is recommended to put very little value on avoidance relative to EHP. 
This is because avoidance does not synergize well with how damage intake and healing for tanks function in vanilla raids. 
Incoming damage from raid bosses is extremely spikey in vanilla, and it is simply impossible to stack enough EHP to survive more than a few consecutive damage spikes from hard-hitting bosses even when gearing for mitigation. 
This means that as a tank, you can never rely on dodges to keep you alive when you don't have enough Stamina or Armor to do so. 
As a result, spam healing tanks and accepting a large amount of overhealing as a consequence is standard practice amongst competitive healing teams in vanilla. 
Getting in a lucky dodge once in a while does not help these healing teams out whatsoever, as they need to be spam healing you regardless to avoid a death from a bad RNG spike, so the dodge just turns into more overhealing.

- Defense:
Defense serves three functions for Feral Druids when tanking raid bosses: 
(1) It increases the tank's change to dodge boss attacks, 
(2) It increases the chance that the boss misses the tank entirely, and 
(3) It reduces the chance that the tank is critically hit. 
Both (1) and (2) increase the total avoidance of the tank, with every 12.5 Defense Skill providing 1% total avoidance. 
Therefore, there is a direct equivalency between Dodge Chance and Defense for avoidance.

- Armor:
Besides avoidance, Armor is the only source of physical damage mitigation for Feral tanks, since bears cannot block or parry. 
The relationship between damage mitigation and Armor is r = A / (A + 5755), where r is the fraction of incoming damage that is mitigated, and A is the fully raid buffed Armor value in Dire Bear Form. 
For example, a tank with 9,000 Armor mitigates 61% of incoming physical damage. 
In Classic WoW, damage mitigation is capped at 75%, which corresponds to an Armor cap of 17.265 against a level 63 raid boss. 
Each 1 Armor on a gear piece actually provides 4.6 armor when tanking due to the multiplier from Dire Bear Form, and this can be further boosted to 4.784 Armor if you spec into Thick Hide as part of the standard main tank build.
